# docs/

The July 2026 assessments that used to live here — the drift report, the CTO review, the Clerk instance audit, the state-and-roadmap handoff, the implementation plan and the build plan — are **internal working documents of Samouraï Coop** since 2026-08-23 and are kept outside this repository. They were written while the service was being built; an assessment of a live service is a map of what was weak at the time, and that does not belong in a public repository after the fact.

Nothing in them is needed to deploy, operate or audit the service: the operational knowledge they produced lives in [`RUNBOOK.md`](../RUNBOOK.md) (the silent-failure traps, one section each), [`DEPLOY.md`](../DEPLOY.md), the gate scripts in [`scripts/`](../scripts/) and their comments, and the templates in [`deploy/`](../deploy/). The transparency claim on the legal pages refers to exactly that: the configuration, the gates and the theme of the running service are public here, in full.
